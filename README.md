# Awesome-In-App-Learning-Enablement

## Top In-App Learning & Enablement Tools Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Digital Adoption, Guided Onboarding & In-App Training*  

**Last updated: March 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **In-App Learning & Enablement Tools**. These tools embed guidance, tours, checklists, and training directly inside applications — helping users learn software in context, reducing support burden, and accelerating onboarding without leaving the workflow.



**Examples** include Salesforce Learning Paths, Spekit, WalkMe, Whatfix, Pendo, Lessonly by Seismic, Mindtickle, Brainshark, Showpad Coach, and Highspot Training (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom tour logic, and transparent user data — ideal for developer-led teams, privacy-conscious organizations, and teams avoiding per-MAU pricing that scales expensively.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Salesforce Learning Paths](https://www.salesforce.com/)**  

  Guided learning and enablement within Salesforce ecosystem for onboarding users to CRM workflows and best practices.



- **[Spekit](https://www.spekit.com/)**  

  In-app learning and enablement platform with digital adoption, knowledge reinforcement, and change management capabilities.



- **[WalkMe](https://www.walkme.com/)**  

  Enterprise Digital Adoption Platform (DAP) with guided tours, process automation, and analytics. Note: Real cost includes maintenance effort — teams report needing 2-3 FTEs to maintain complex deployments . Adds 200-500KB to page weight .



- **[Whatfix](https://whatfix.com/)**  

  Digital adoption platform with in-app guidance, self-help widgets, and process automation. Offers enterprise on-premise deployment for strict data sovereignty requirements .



- **[Pendo](https://www.pendo.io/)**  

  Product analytics and in-app guidance platform with tours, tooltips, and user onboarding capabilities.



- **[Lessonly by Seismic](https://www.seismic.com/)**  

  Sales training and enablement platform with practice, coaching, and knowledge reinforcement.



- **[Mindtickle](https://www.mindtickle.com/)**  

  Sales readiness platform with onboarding, training, and coaching in a unified system.



- **[Brainshark](https://www.brainshark.com/)**  

  Sales readiness and coaching platform with video practice and content management.



- **[Showpad Coach](https://www.showpad.com/)**  

  Sales enablement platform with training, coaching, and content activation.



- **[Highspot Training](https://www.highspot.com/)**  

  Sales enablement platform with training, coaching, and content guidance.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Shepherd.js](https://github.com/shipshapecode/shepherd)**  

  Open-source, fully featured digital adoption platform (DAP) and user onboarding library for creating custom tours, trainings, and announcements. Supports React, Ember, Angular, Vue.js, and plain JavaScript. **Dual-licensed**: AGPL-3.0 for open source/non-commercial use, with a commercial license required for commercial products . 12.6K+ GitHub stars .



- **[Usertour](https://github.com/usertour/usertour)**  

  Open-source user onboarding platform positioned as an alternative to Userflow and Appcues. Create in-app product tours, checklists, and surveys in minutes with full control. TypeScript-based, 1,444+ GitHub stars . Available via Docker Hub .



- **[Tour Kit](https://github.com/domidex01/tour-kit)**  

  Developer library for code-first onboarding with composable packages. **MIT core (free)** with Pro features at $99 one-time. Under 8KB gzipped. Provides tours, checklists, hints, and analytics. Designed for teams wanting to avoid MAU pricing that makes mid-market SaaS tools expensive at scale .



- **[React Joyride](https://github.com/gilbarbara/react-joyride)**  

  Production-ready React component for creating guided tours and onboarding flows. 603K weekly npm downloads, powering production onboarding at thousands of companies . Works with Next.js App Router (requires `'use client'` directive) .



- **[Driver.js](https://github.com/kamranahmedse/driver.js)**  

  Lightweight (3KB) open-source library for creating product tours and onboarding flows. Lacks built-in analytics but is extremely efficient for simple guidance needs .



- **[Laudspeaker](https://github.com/laudspeaker/laudspeaker)**  

  Open-source customer engagement and product onboarding platform. Alternative to Braze, OneSignal, Customer.io, Appcues, and Pendo. Design product onboarding flows and send event-triggered emails, SMS, push, and more. TypeScript-based, 2,216+ GitHub stars .



- **[Project Wingman (ClientLens)](https://github.com/avinashgaurav/project-wingman-ClientLens)**  

  Open-source AI sales copilot for browser sidebar with live call coaching and pitches grounded in your own knowledge base. Self-hosted on your own LLM keys — no vendor server. Features pre-call pitch generation, live coaching during Google Meet calls, and post-call summaries pushed to CRM. Python 3.11+ required .



- **[UDAN Agent](https://github.com/Digital-Assistant/Digital_Assistant_Client)**  

  Open-source AI-powered Digital Adoption Platform (DAP) using generative AI for accessibility. Features AI-powered accessibility, seamless integration, crowdsourced knowledge base of user-created "sequences" (step-by-step guides), and extensible architecture for custom skills. AGPL licensed, Gitcoin-funded .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[Project Wingman Backend](https://github.com/avinashgaurav/project-wingman)** — Self-hosted AI sales coaching infrastructure with five live agents (sentiment, agenda pacing, coaching, objection handling, validation) .

- **[Userpilot Alternatives](https://repos.ecosyste.ms/hosts/GitHub/topics/userpilot)** — Curated collection of open-source user onboarding tools .

- **[Open-source DAP Tools](https://repos.ecosyste.ms/hosts/GitHub/topics/product-adoption)** — GitHub topic aggregating product adoption and onboarding projects .

- **Developer Libraries** — Shepherd.js, React Joyride, Driver.js, Tour Kit for web-only guidance (most lack mobile SDKs) .

- **Feedback & Survey Tools** — Formbricks, OpenForms for in-app feedback collection.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Shepherd.js** or **Usertour** for tour and onboarding flows, **Laudspeaker** for event-triggered engagement, **Tour Kit** for lightweight composable guidance, and **Project Wingman** for AI-powered sales coaching to build a complete self-hosted in-app learning stack.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- In-app learning tools must comply with data protection regulations (GDPR, CCPA), accessibility standards, and licensing terms (especially AGPL vs MIT for commercial use).

- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper security hardening, client-side data handling, and ongoing maintenance.
